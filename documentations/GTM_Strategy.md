## Strategy

### Phase 1 — Listen & Mine (Weeks 1–2)

- Join all Tier 1 and Tier 2 communities. Don't post yet.
- Search keywords: *'WhatsApp clients', 'client check-ins', 'managing online clients', 'CRM for coaches', 'client management headache'*
- Save 20–30 posts/threads where coaches describe the exact pain FitFlow solves
- Note the exact language coaches use — steal these phrases for messaging and blog titles
- Identify the top 5–10 most active contributors in each community

### Phase 2 — Comment & Contribute (Weeks 3–6)

- 5 genuine comments per day across communities — answer questions, share systems, no links
- When someone complains about the exact problem FitFlow solves, offer a detailed solution (without pitching)
- Build a credibility baseline: 20–30 helpful comments before you mention FitFlow at all
- DM 2–3 coaches per week who expressed the pain point directly — offer a free concierge setup
- In Reddit: respond to 'what tools do you use for X' threads with honest comparisons

### Phase 3 — Soft Introduce (Weeks 7–10)

- Post value-first content (templates, systems, scripts) in Facebook groups — no pitch
- In Reddit AMAs or 'what's working' threads, mention FitFlow naturally as something you're building/testing
- Share a case study post: *'How a coach managing 40 clients cut WhatsApp chaos by doing X'* — link to blog
- Post in Tier 2 groups: *'Building a tool for this exact problem, looking for 5 coaches to test for free'*
- Convert 3–5 engaged commenters into concierge beta users

### Phase 4 — Content Engine (Weeks 11+)

- Publish 2 blog posts per week optimised for coach search terms — share snippets in communities
- Run a free resource drop (e.g. '22 WhatsApp message templates for coaches') as a lead magnet
- Post monthly 'ask me anything' style threads in Tier 1 groups about coach ops and client retention
- Convert blog content into community posts — one blog = one Facebook post + one Reddit comment thread
- Build a backlink profile: submit to directories (Product Hunt, G2, Capterra, Indie Hackers) in Week 2

---

## Hard Rules

- Never post a link in a community comment until you have 20+ helpful comments of history
- Never pitch FitFlow directly — let it come up naturally in response to a problem
- Facebook group admins will ban you for direct self-promotion. Always lead with value.
- Reddit: never use the same account for both 'asking for tool advice' and 'recommending FitFlow'
- One soft mention of FitFlow per week max in early phases — quality over frequency
